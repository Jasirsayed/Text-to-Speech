    Thirteen languages, ten scripts.

    Dravidian, Indo-Aryan and Tibeto-Burman languages, each with jointly trained male and female voices.

    How a sentence becomes speech

    01 · Normalize

    Text front-end

    Numbers are expanded to words, sentences are segmented, and English words are transliterated into the native script.

    02 · FastPitch

    Acoustic model

    A non-autoregressive model predicts pitch and duration per character, then renders a mel-spectrogram for the chosen speaker.

    03 · HiFi-GAN

    Neural vocoder

    The spectrogram is turned into a waveform, denoised, and returned to your browser as WAV.
